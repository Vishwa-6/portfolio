SupportNest

Business AI Customer Support Platform

Overview

SupportNest is a Micro-SaaS customer support platform that allows any business to transform their static company knowledge (pricing, policies, services, working hours, FAQs) into an interactive 24/7 intelligent AI support assistant with zero technical setup required.

⚠️ The Business Problem
  • Small businesses can't afford 24/7 dedicated support teams.
  • Repetitive customer queries consume over 60% of daily resources.
  • Slow response times lead to high customer drop-off and lost sales.
✨ The SupportNest Solution
  • Instant onboarding with dynamic knowledge base ingestion.
  • Auto-generated unique chatbot share links & QR codes.
  • Context-aware, zero-hallucination responses based on uploaded data.

How It Works (Workflow)

01
Register & Setup

Business creates an account and sets up their support profile.

02
Upload Knowledge

Feed business FAQs, services, and policies into the system.

03
Generate Chatbot & QR

Platform produces a dedicated link and scannable QR code.

04
24/7 Instant Support

Customers get real-time answers; owners review conversation logs.

Technology & Architecture

Python FastAPI AI & LLM Ingestion RAG Architecture Vector Embeddings QR Code Generator RESTful APIs Cloud Ready
// Cloud Project · FinOps & AI

FinOps Sentinel

AI Cloud Cost Detective & Waste Analyzer

Overview

FinOps Sentinel is an open-source, AI-powered AWS cost intelligence engine. It leverages a hybrid architecture: a deterministic Python rules engine to audit cloud infrastructure waste across 12 AWS resource types with 100% accuracy, paired with Google Gemini AI to automatically generate step-by-step remediation plans.

⚠️ The Cloud Cost Problem
  • Over 30% of cloud spend is wasted on idle/unattached resources.
  • Orphaned EBS volumes, unattached Elastic IPs, and idle NAT gateways generate silent bills.
  • Engineers lack automated, risk-free audits with clear remediation steps.
✨ The FinOps Sentinel Solution
  • 16 Deterministic Rules across 4 severity tiers (Critical to Low).
  • Hybrid AI Architecture: Deterministic detection + AI remediation.
  • Strict Least Privilege: 100% read-only scan (zero risk to production).

Detection & Auditing Capabilities

COMPUTE
EC2 & NAT Gateways

Detects idle instances (CPU < 5%), oversized dev instances, and 0-traffic NAT gateways.

STORAGE
EBS & Snapshots

Finds unattached volumes, costly io1 tiers, and orphaned snapshots older than 90 days.

DATABASE
RDS & DynamoDB

Audits over-provisioned storage (> 80% free), oversized instances, and high RCU/WCU.

REAL-TIME
WebSockets & UI

Live scan progress over WebSockets, donut charts, CSV export, and scan history in SQLite.

Technology & Architecture

Python Boto3 (AWS SDK) FastAPI Google Gemini AI WebSockets SQLite / SQLAlchemy Deterministic Engine JWT / Bcrypt AWS IAM Security
